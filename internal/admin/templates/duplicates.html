Duplicates

Tracks the iOS app would collapse as copies of the same recording, grouped by the client's own identity rule and tiered by evidence. Suppression changes what is served — nothing is ever deleted or moved; the bridge has no code path that modifies library files.

Serving policy

Applies immediately (no restart): saving re-runs the stamping pass. Copies that stop being suppressed re-sync to devices on their next delta; copies that become suppressed disappear for new pairings and full rescans, and stay hidden by the app's own de-duplication until then. The inconclusive tier is never suppressed under any policy.

Tier names are evidence claims, not verdicts: different-format members have different sample rates, bit depths or codecs — different masters, not redundant copies. different-audio members share geometry but their FLAC audio checksums differ — proven remasters, never suppressed. identical-audio is checksum-proven bit-identical audio, the only tier where redundancy is a fact. self-nested is the one pure filesystem fact (the same file uploaded at multiple nesting depths, e.g. CD 01/CD 01/CD 01/…) — fix those at the storage source. inconclusive absorbs every uncertainty.
