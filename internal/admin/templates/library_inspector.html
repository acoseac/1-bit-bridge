Library Inspector

Browse the indexed library and trigger upscaling at folder / root / whole-library granularity. Click a folder to enter it; use the ⓘ button on a row to see the upscale projection without navigating. The bridge handles the work in the background; paired iOS devices pick up upscaled variants automatically when their "Prefer upscaled audio" toggle is on.
