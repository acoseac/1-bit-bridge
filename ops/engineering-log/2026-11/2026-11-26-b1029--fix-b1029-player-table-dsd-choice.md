## 2026-11-26 — B1029: the Player page's transports table says the DAC's DSD choice and its converted rates

### Why

At 78ea8003 the console's Player page set each DSD row of its transports table from the boot snapshot's legacy keys: `consolePlayer.transports` (`cmd/bridge/serve_player_console.go` 515 to 523 there) worded the DoP row `dsdMode <mode>, allowDoP <bool>` and the native row `dsdMode <mode>` from `bootDSDMode` and `bootAllowDoP`, which `newBridgeOutput` took from `output.player` (`cmd/bridge/serve_player_output.go` 132 there). Since the player's DSD cutover (B728 B7) those keys decide nothing, and a fresh install's DSD state holds auto for a DAC with no choice of its own, so there the rows said `dsdMode off` while DSD played. The rows listed the published set's native and DoP tuples alone (535 to 550 there): a rate the set converts to PCM, which GET /v1/outputs publishes as `dsdPlayback.converted` (`serve_player_output.go` 218 to 221 there), was on no row, so a DAC that plays DSD only converted showed two DSD rows that played nothing.

### What the table reads now

On 08a00eee, each of the three facts a row rests on, and its lines:

- Set to, on the DoP, native and converted rows: `dsdChoiceText` (`serve_player_console.go` 589 to 608), the choice GET /v1/outputs publishes for the bound DAC, from the facts `transports` is handed: `dsdMode` (`facts.DSDMode`, `serve_player_output.go` 176), with `dsdDAC.choice`'s cap and its revision, which tells the DAC's own choice from the bridge's default; on the DoP row `allowDoP` (`facts.AllowDoP`: a DoP rate the profile heard or states, or the owner's confirmation, `internal/player/playback/dsd_view.go` 219 to 221), never `dsdDAC.choice.dopConfirmed`, which a profile that heard DoP leaves false (`serve_player_console.go` 519 to 527); and `none published` for a player GET /v1/outputs does not list, one serve did not start or one starting.
- Rates (the column was Approved, and since the cutover nothing approves a tuple): the set's tuples on the DoP and native rows as before, and each converted rate on a fourth row, converted, with the PCM it becomes, its rate and bits (566 to 568); the PCM row's, every rate the DAC takes. No converted rate is listed on the DoP, native or PCM row.
- The DAC takes it, on the converted row: the bound DAC's published convert option, a PCM target its altsets take (532 to 542), unknown where no read side is published.

Plays is the output open and a rate listed, on every row (575), so a player turned off or a DAC gone plays nothing while its facts stay published. The page's hint and headings say what the rows rest on (`internal/admin/templates/output.html` 96 and 99), the DoP note says when the player sends DoP (`serve_player_console.go` 554 to 555), the renderer labels the fourth row DSD converted to PCM (`internal/admin/static/app.js` 8111), and `bootDSDMode`, `bootAllowDoP` and the comment beside them are gone from `serve_player_output.go`, nothing else having read them. The page still offers no DSD control, command or write route: `TestThePlayerPageOffersNoDSDControl` passes unchanged.
