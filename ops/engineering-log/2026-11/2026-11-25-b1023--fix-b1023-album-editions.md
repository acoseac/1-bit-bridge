## 2026-11-25 — B1023: an album whose folders hold the same songs shows one edition at a time

### The hazard

The catalog's album is its tags' (`dupes.AlbumIDOf(dupes.Resolve(row))`, the phone's identity), so a DSD rip, a hi-res download and a CD rip of one release, each in a folder of its own, are one album. Nothing on the server removes the copies: DSD and PCM are never cross-suppressed, and a PCM pair of different formats only under `highest-quality` with a conclusive group. The album's rows come in the catalog's order (resolved disc, track, path), so the copies of each song sit together, and the album page listed every row: on a box seen at 0.2.3, 30 rows for 13 songs (13 DSF at DSD64 with no disc tag, 13 FLAC 192/24 and 4 FLAC 44.1/16 tagged disc 1). `trackList` drew a disc heading only at a tagged row whose disc changed, so the untagged DSF row of track 1 stood above "Disc 1"; Play, Shuffle and Add to queue took all 30 rows (the bridge's player was sent the album's id, which the server expands to every row), and the count line, the disabled rule, the stat line and Delete counted them all.

### What changed

All in the console (`static/player/views.js`, one export in `format.js`, `player.css`); no Go production file, no route and no field of the album's JSON: each row already carries its path, its disc and track tags, its title, codec, rate and depth, `isDsd`, `play` with a copy's geometry, and `bridge.way`.

- **Editions.** `albumEditions` takes a row's folder by the catalog's own fold, its path's parent or that parent's parent where the parent is a disc folder by `dupes.discNumberForFolderName`'s rule (the whole name, cd, disc, disk, vol or volume in any case, a number whose last one or two digits past its zeros are the disc, in a path of three parts or more), never the folder-art rule. An album is in editions only where a song repeats across its folders: two rows of two folders with the same resolved disc (tag, else disc folder, else 1) and the same nonempty name (the title, else the file's name past a leading track number, lowercased, every run of other than letters and digits one space). A track number is never the test: the scanner writes the number it reads from a file's name into the same field a tag fills (`fillTrackNumberFromFilename`), so two untagged folders whose first files are `01. Overture` and `01. Finale` would have split one release. A leading number is dropped from a file's name alone, never from a title (`7 Seconds` stays itself). An album no song of which repeats is one edition and draws as before.
- **Words and order.** An edition's format is the one most of its rows share (`dsdName` for DSD, `formatChip` else, a tie to the better), its words its format and count (`DSD64 · 13`, named `DSD64, 13 tracks`); where two read alike, each adds its folder's name, and where the names match too, the shortest end of its path that tells it apart. The options are in one order on every output: DSD by its 1-bit rate, then PCM by depth and rate, then the rest; then more rows; then the folder.
- **The default.** `bestFor` compares, term by term: whether any row plays on the output chosen; the key most of its playing rows share, from `format.js`'s `rankFor`, which asks `playFor` and `resolvePlayable` (on the bridge's player native, then DoP, then PCM, then converted, which is never bit-exact, a DSD way by its 1-bit rate and PCM by depth then rate; in this browser its own file before a copy, so an own lossy file before a lossless copy, then the depth and the rate of what plays, the row's or its copy's `variantBits` and `variantRateHz`), or its format's where none plays; how many play; how many rows; then the fewer folders, the shorter path and the one that sorts first (`dupes.outranks`' tail). For the album above: FLAC 192/24 in this browser, DSD64 on the bridge's player where DSD64 plays natively or by DoP, FLAC 192/24 where it converts or does not play DSD64, DSD64 where no row says how the bridge's player plays it.
- **The slot.** `editionShown`, one slot for the page's life keyed by the album and the output, as `detailTabs`' tab memory is, holds the edition shown, picked or the default: a redraw for a variant job or a decode failure keeps it, and another album, a change of output, a reload and a link opened afresh show the default.
- **The picker.** A group in the album's head, between the stat line and the count line, named by its word Edition, a toggle button an edition; built once a render, so a pick moves `aria-pressed` alone and the pressed option keeps the focus. A pick repaints the stat line, the count line and controls, and the Tracks panel in place from the album fetched (no request, no `player:rerender`, no `route()`); a mixed album's source names are read once a render, so the source filter repaints with nothing to wait for; a pick on a page the route has left paints nothing. Each option is 44 px square at the least under `(pointer: coarse)`.
- **What follows the edition.** Play, Shuffle, Add to queue and a row's own Play take the edition shown; on the bridge's player a multi-edition album is sent as the edition's paths (`{tracks}`, no `album`), an album of one edition by its id as before. An edition past the control's bounds (20,000 paths of 1 to 4,096 bytes, 8 MiB in all, a KiB left to the rest of the body) has Play and Add to queue disabled, described by words that say to play it in this browser or to add its tracks from their rows: the control's own refusal says to send a playlist by its id, which an edition has none of. The count line, the disabled rule and the stat line are the edition's (its discs, tracks, duration and size, an SACD image's size counted once, its format in the chip, the album's year). Delete reads the settings once a render, keeps an edition's deletion on the page while it runs (a pick away and back shows `Deleting…`, never a live Delete over rows already sent), takes the paths the trash moved off the page when it answers, names the edition in its confirmation (`Move 4 files of the FLAC 44.1/16 edition of "<album>" to the trash?`; an album of one edition keeps its words) and leaves out an SACD image's tracks, which are no files. A row's heading disc is its tag, else its disc folder's number, else 1 where any row of its edition has either, on every album page, so no untagged row stands above Disc 1.
- **B1021's failed sources** stand as they were: the edition code reads playability through `playFor` and `resolvePlayable` alone, never writes or clears the failed set, and leaves `failures` and `redrawnFor` alone; the watcher reads the rows of the edition shown, each paint's, so a failure in an edition picked after the page was drawn redraws it too.

The rules entry is `admin-console.md`'s line after B1021's disabled-controls line, its long form in `ops/History-rules-admin-console.md`; that B1021 line now reads "the edition an album shows" and "the output's pick alone", in both files.

### The decisions taken

The item's open decisions, each as its default unless said: an album is in editions only where a song repeats across its folders, a song matched by its resolved disc and name alone (the track number dropped from the match, for the scanner's backfill above); PCM above converted on the bridge's player; PCM by depth, then rate; a group of toggle buttons named Edition, each its format and count; one slot keyed by album and output; a label's folder name where two read alike, the shortest end of the path where the names match; Delete takes the edition shown and names it; the stat line states the edition's discs, tracks, duration, size and format, the year the album's; one heading rule on every album page; one option order on every output; quality before completeness.

Where the item asked that a disc folder parse to a disc of at least 1, so that `CD0` stay unfolded: the catalog's fold folds `CD0` to disc 0 (`discNumberForFolderName` keeps a run of zeros as 0, and `effectiveAlbumPath` folds it), so the console does too, and the shared table holds `CD0` folding to disc 0 on both sides; a change there would be the catalog's and the console's together.

### What stays open

- The Variants tab, About and the booklet stay album-wide: a Generate covers every edition, the variant routes taking album ids.
- Everything but the album page sees the album whole: the album grid's counts and chips, the artist page, search, mixes, playlists and the iOS app.
- Copies within one folder stay one edition, interleaved as before, and so do copies with no name in common (a folder whose titles are its file names with their numbers matches only folders whose file names agree).
- An SACD image's stereo tracks are an edition of their own, beside a file edition; an image offers no Delete, as its tracks are no files.
- A folder that repeats no song beside other editions is an edition of its own (a bonus folder beside two editions).
- A disc tag of 0 reads as untagged, since the wire omits it.
- A row's own Play on the bridge's player sends the list it was drawn in, the edition's, as it always sent the album's rows, with no bound of the page's.
