### What was built

`internal/player/dsdchime` (`chime.go`). Its exported surface, all PR 3 needs (W4):

```go
type Chime struct{ /* unexported */ }
func Get(ctx context.Context, bitRateHz int) (*Chime, error)
func (c *Chime) Format() format.NativeDSD
func (c *Chime) ReadAt(dst [][]byte, off int64) (int, error)
```

`Get` takes DSD64, DSD128 or DSD256 and answers that rate's chime, built on the rate's first use
and kept for the process. `Format` is the stream, `format.NativeDSD` with the rate, two channels
and the samples a channel, no container (the chime is made, not demuxed). `ReadAt` copies each
channel's canonical bytes (bit 7 earliest, `internal/player/format/pcm.go:96-110`) from `off` into
`dst[0]` and `dst[1]`, as many as both hold and the chime has left, answering `io.EOF` with fewer.
That is how the view fits §6.2 (S5): the check prepares the chime under its hold and plays it
through the DSD path under test, which reads a `decode.DSDDecoder` (`ReadDSD` into two planes,
`Seek`); PR 3's adapter is a cursor over `ReadAt`, as `listen_test.go`'s `chimeSource` is, so a
check copies the cached planes chunk by chunk into its own buffers, allocates nothing once warm,
and cannot write into the cache. The errors stay unexported: PR 3 admits only the rates the
effective set plays, and any error of `Get` is a preparation that failed.

The programme, at each rate: 0.5 s of `0x69`; 0.7 s of 880 Hz on the left, the right `0x69`; 0.7 s
of 1320 Hz on the right, the left `0x69`; 0.5 s of `0x69`. Each tone's 0.7 s is 10 ms of zero
input, the tone with its 20 ms raised-cosine rise and fall, and a tail of zero input. The tones are
sines at phase zero at their start.

- **The loop.** `tone.modulate` is the generator's `modulate()` line for line: the same integrator
  gains and update order, the same quantizer test (`v >= 0`), each product inside an addition or a
  subtraction converted to `float64` on its own, which forbids a fused multiply-add on arm64; the
  feed-forward weights, all 1, are left out, their products exact. Its input is in the one-bit
  range, so the generator's FULL_SCALE, its 0.5, is the digest test's adapter (a tone of peak 0.5
  with no edges), exact either way, and the chime's one 0.5 is in its level (R3). The quantizer's
  ±1 is made from the decision's bit, with no branch: compiled, `UCOMISD`, `SETCC`, then integer
  `XORQ`, `SHLQ` and `ORQ` (`go build -gcflags=-S`). Over a DSD256 section, bit for bit the same
  either way, the loop took 10.6 ns a step with a branch on that coin toss and 7.9 without one, the
  bound's per-step check included (7.6 without the check).
- **The generator's digest** holds through it: one second at DSD64 of the rotation (cos and sin of
  2π·1000/2822400, `0x1.ffffacda93a18p-1`, `0x1.23ca52113ba91p-9`), unpacked to one byte a sample,
  hashes to `c164f2ef37c9eb01d8c07404b4e81f3297f80e3009f0e7e13e137dbd4f804154` with 1,411,200
  ones, the Python run's (`TestTheChimeLoopIsTheGenerators`, S3).
- **Packed output.** Whole bytes, bit 7 earliest, the sections byte-aligned at every rate (0.5, 1.2
  and 1.9 s); each plane one `make` at its size and the pads filled by doubling copies.
- **Cancellation.** The context is asked at each section's samples 0, 65,536, 131,072 and so on:
  62, 122 and 242 asks for a whole build, counted by `TestTheChimeStopsWithItsContext`, and once
  more by `Get` before it publishes.
- **The bound.** An integrator past 4 ends the modulation with an error, so the build answers that
  error and no chime. The loop holds a sine of 0.8 of the one-bit range with every integrator under
  0.7 (0.6835 at its largest) and runs away from 0.9 on; the chime's integrators peaked at 0.643
  to 0.649 at every rate (a per-step peak in scratch, before the tails were chosen; the test holds
  them under 0.7).
- **The constants** (S4): the level and the nine (cos, sin) pairs (880 Hz, 1320 Hz and the edges' π
  over 20 ms, at each rate) are hex literals, each the float64 nearest its value, from 256-bit
  `math/big` arithmetic (π by Machin's formula, cosine and sine by their series), which the
  architecture test computes again. Six of the nine `math.Sin` and `math.Cos` pairs on this host
  differ from them in the last place. No libm call is made, setup included.
- **The level** (item 6, R3): `level = 0x1.030dc4ea03a72p-6`, 0.5 × 10^(−30/20); its comment says
  it is 6.02 dB below "−30 dBFS of the one-bit full scale" and that a listen on a real DAC is owed
  before any route exposes the check. Through `dsdconv` each tone reads −36.0206 dBFS.

Size: `chime.go` is 330 lines, 221 of code, 87 of comment and 22 blank, against the brief's "about
260": the excess is the cache's contract (`publish` and its deferred keeping, the test's `waiting`
seam), the joins' tails and the per-step bound. The tests are `chime_test.go` (structure, the cache,
the benchmarks) and `listen_test.go` (the measurements through `dsdconv`).

### The joins to `0x69` (R2, S2, V3)

Measured as `sdm.c` measures its own boundary (`window`, `probe_level`, `:629-741`): the running
sum's offsets from its local means over 0.5 ms either side (`rate/2000` samples), against literal
`0x69`'s over the same samples; and the audio band against `0x69`, low-passed at 20 kHz by its
Kaiser-windowed sinc (β 5.65, `rate/2756` taps either side), in dB SA-CD over 10 ms. Every chime
boundary is byte-aligned, where `0x69`'s prefix sums average zero (V3). The bound is the
reference's: within 0.02, at most −100 dB SA-CD.

- **The start.** From rest the loop with no input makes `96 69 69 96` over and over, exactly (8.4
  million samples checked in scratch): 32 samples, DC-free, its spectrum at multiples of the rate
  over 32. The 10 ms lead-in is that pattern, so the join to the pad before it reads 0x69's own
  probe floor: −105.9, −112.3 and −118.3 dB SA-CD at DSD64, DSD128 and DSD256, against pure
  `0x69`'s −106.3, −112.3 and −118.3; offsets 0.0000 to 0.0007.
- **The end.** A tone's end is where the loop's state is what the tone left. With the shortest
  tail (20 ms of zero input) the 1320 Hz tone's join read −98.0 dB SA-CD at DSD64 and −98.5 at
  DSD128, its offsets 0.0000: the click is the loop's own in-band idle noise, which at DSD64 sits
  near the bound. In scratch (oscillators by `math.Sin`): with no tail the 880 Hz ends read −95.9 and
  −97.5 dB SA-CD at DSD64 and DSD128, and over tail lengths of 0 to 60 ms in 0.5 ms steps 10 of
  120 (880 Hz) and 17 of 120 (1320 Hz) passed at DSD64, 85 of 120 at DSD128 and all 60 tried at
  DSD256 (0 to 30 ms).
  Seeded TPDF dither in the last 10 ms, `sdm.c`'s treatment, was refused on its measurement: at DSD64
  no seed of 300 passed at a peak of 0.4 (median −79.4), 0.1 (−85.8) or 0.02 (−87.6 dB SA-CD), the
  dithered loop's own in-band noise standing over the bound.
- **The treatment.** Each tone's tail is 20 ms plus the first whole number of 1/44,100 s frames,
  from none, whose join stands within 0.01 of `0x69`'s offset and at most −101 dB SA-CD (a decibel
  inside the bound), searched with the production loop and held in `rates`: 0 and 4 frames at
  DSD64 (880 and 1320 Hz), 0 and 1 at DSD128, 0 and 0 at DSD256. Deterministic, with no seed:
  `TestTheChimesJoinsToSilenceAreClean` searches again and requires the table's answer.
- **Every join** (pad to tone and tone to pad, both channels, every rate; offsets before, after,
  and the click): DSD64 0.5 s left 0.0000, 0.0000, −105.9; 1.2 s left −104.0, right −105.9; 1.9 s
  right −102.6. DSD128 0.5 s left 0.0000, −0.0007, −112.3; 1.2 s left −108.6, right −112.3; 1.9 s
  right +0.0007, 0.0000, −106.6. DSD256 0.5 s left −118.3; 1.2 s left −110.2, right −118.3; 1.9 s
  right −117.1. A channel `0x69` on both sides of a boundary reads `0x69`'s floor, offsets 0.0000.

### The in-band floor (O2, V1, W2)

`TestTheChimesInBandResidualIsUnderItsFloor`, its chain stated in its doc: the chime converted by
`internal/player/dsdconv` under the 176.4 kHz 24-bit plan (V1: a 16-bit plan's TPDF would swamp
DSD128's and DSD256's floors); each tone's section's 0.1 to 0.6 s, its steady middle, 88,200
samples, 440 and 660 whole periods; a sinusoid at the known frequency and a constant fitted by
least squares and taken away; the residual windowed by the four-term Blackman-Harris window,
zero-padded to 2^17 points, each bin's power normalised by the window's energy (2|X|²/(N·Σw²)), and
the bins from 20 Hz to 20 kHz summed.

| | 880 Hz | 1320 Hz | floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| DSD64 | −75.10 dBFS, 39.08 dB under | −73.28 dBFS, 37.26 dB under | 35 |
| DSD128 | −106.59, 70.57 | −104.86, 68.84 | 65 |
| DSD256 | −136.72, 100.70 | −138.94, 102.92 | 95 |

Within 0.12 dB of O2's figures at DSD64 and DSD128; at DSD256 1.0 and 1.8 dB under them, still
5.7 and 7.9 dB inside the floor. The cause at DSD64 is the loop's noise transfer function, (z−1)^5 over
(z−1)^5 + 0.4(z−1)^4 + 0.08(z−1)^3 + 0.008(z−1)^2 + 0.0004(z−1) + 0.000008 by its update order, its
five zeros at DC: −64.3, −47.8 and −36.8 dB at 10, 15 and 20 kHz at DSD64, −64.3 at 20 kHz at
DSD128 and −93.6 at DSD256.

Through the same chain (`TestTheChimeIsModulatedAtItsOwnRate`): every tone reads 880.0000 or
1320.0000 Hz (the largest bin of the windowed spectrum zero-padded to 2^19 points, refined by the
parabola through its dB magnitude and its neighbours'), within 1 Hz, and −36.0206 dBFS, within
0.5 dB of the intended −36.0206; the channel silent meanwhile, its DC taken off, has no in-band
power at all (`0x69` converts to exact zeros there), so it lies more than 60 dB under (W2). Against
each lower rate's chime with its samples repeated, 0.500 of a rate's samples differ, on both
channels, counted over the whole plane: its pads and its silent channel, 70.8 % of it, are `0x69` in
both chimes and differ from themselves repeated in half their bits, so a tone section repeated whole
would leave 0.354 of the plane differing, not none, and one repeated in its first half about 0.43,
over the test's 0.4 (corrected in place on 2026-11-21 by bridge lows 14: this said a repetition
would differ in none; the test counts each tone's section alone since, and 0.4996 to 0.5002 of a
section's samples differ).

The edges (S1's check): each 20 ms rise and fall, the converter's samples less the intended
programme, windowed alike over 2^16 points, its largest in-band component: DSD64 45.2 and 44.5 dB
under the tone (880 Hz rise and fall), 41.2 and 41.1 (1320 Hz); DSD128 73.9 and 72.9, 73.0 and 71.8;
DSD256 106.6 and 105.4, 102.5 and 106.3. The edges' in-band residual: −70.2 to −71.5 dBFS at
DSD64, −100.7 to −101.8 at DSD128, −133.9 to −135.3 at DSD256.

The rotation's drift (S4), by the loop's own recurrence over a section's 7,902,720 steps at DSD256:
the radius strays from 1 by 8.37e-11 (880 Hz) and 4.27e-10 (1320 Hz) at most, 3.7e-9 dB.

### The cache's contract (R4, S5)

Stated in the package doc and tested on a cache of the test's own with a stand-in build (`cache`
takes its build as a field, and a `waiting` hook, nil in production, hears each caller begin to wait
for another's build): concurrent first callers cause one build and one publication, every caller
having the one chime (`TestConcurrentFirstCallersShareOneBuild`); a waiter's own cancellation
answers it at once while the build it waited for goes on for the rest
(`TestAWaitersCancellationEndsOnlyItsWait`); a build its caller's context ended publishes nothing,
and a caller that waited for it builds again (`TestACancelledBuildPublishesNothing`), as does one
whose context ended after the build's last ask and before its publication
(`TestACancellationBeforePublicationPublishesNothing`); and a caller's buffer written after a read
leaves the cache's bytes and the golden as they were (`TestNoCallerCanChangeTheChime`). The real
build behind a fresh cache is cancelled at its third ask and then built again by the next caller
(`TestTheChimeStopsWithItsContext`).
