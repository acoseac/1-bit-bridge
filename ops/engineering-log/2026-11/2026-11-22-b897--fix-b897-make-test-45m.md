## 2026-11-22 — B897: `make test` takes 45 minutes per package

Branch `fix/b897-make-test-45m`, from main `f899d811`.

### The hazard, measured again

`make test` is the local gate: `go test -race` over whole packages, 30 minutes each. On 2026-11-21 a
gate run on a loaded Linux test box took 1,740.8 s for `cmd/bridge` (97 % of 1,800 s), so a timeout
there was the host's, not a hang. CI does not share it: its race suite runs slices of packages
(`.github/scripts/test-shard.sh`, 30 minutes per slice), and its native legs run without `-race`. On
the eight `main` gate runs of 2026-11-21 and 2026-11-22, the native legs' slowest package was
`cmd/bridge` on `windows-latest` at 714 to 1,167 s (at most 65 % of 30 minutes; `internal/backup`
487 to 692 s, `internal/manifest` 550 to 822 s), and on `macos-latest` at 384 to 507 s.

### The change

- `Makefile`'s `test` recipe: `-timeout 45m` for `-timeout 30m`, with its comment saying why and that
  a genuine deadlock now reports 15 minutes later;
- the build-command census's entry for that recipe (`scripts/third-party-licenses/tags.go`);
- `AGENTS.md`'s two mentions of the cap;
- comments only in `.github/workflows/gate.yml` and `.github/scripts/test-shard.sh`, which said the
  shards and the native legs use `make test`'s timeout: they keep 30 minutes, and now say why.

Unchanged: the shards' `-race -timeout 30m` (and their census check), the native legs'
`-timeout 30m` and their job's 60 minutes. The split that would have kept 30 minutes for every
package but the slowest (the branch `fix/b897-make-test-headroom-v2`) is set aside; its design is
backlog B931.
