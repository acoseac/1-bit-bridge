## 2026-11-24 — B1015: a start the reader did not ask for never plays, and the bar's control does what it shows

### What

Three ways the player bar's browser half started, or kept playing, what the reader had not asked
for, each through the gate, on main at `4560b06b` (bridge 0.2.4's tag blocker):

- a Stop, a Pause or a row on the lock screen while a switch to this browser was outstanding (a
  pick of this browser made after a barrier went unconfirmed, so its switch-out takes the bridge's
  queue): the switch's answer still passed `ours()`, took the bridge's queue over the one the
  reader had just cleared or chosen, and played it; the switch's record read back after a lost
  answer, and after a reload, did the same;
- a resume of the browser's own made after a Pause, lost: with an earlier resume of the browser's
  own and the Pause's event still queued, that earlier resume's protective pause was counted as the
  reader's pause, ran `readerAction` and cleared the wait of the resume made after the Pause, which
  its own play event then found paused;
- the bar's Play/Pause over an element an interruption had paused: the label still showed Pause,
  read from `state.playing`, while `toggle` tested `el.paused` and asked the gate for a start; the
  lock screen's Play shared `toggle` and could pause an element whose play event had not come.

This entry's design was written, and committed, before any production edit; the code implements
it.

### The design

**One reader generation.** `readerGen` counts what the reader does to playback. It lives in
`output.js`'s instance, beside `pickGen` and apart from it: `pickGen` moves only with a pick, and
moving it with every reader action would make `ours()` refuse the phase and output moves of a
switch the reader did not undo. It is carried on `currentOutput()`: `readerGen()` reads it, and
`readerActed()` moves it and stores the identity at once (below). `audio.js` reaches it there, as
it already reaches `refusalShown`: its `readerAction()` calls `readerActed()`, and a `readerGen()`
of its own reads the output's, 0 where none is installed (a boot that failed before it, where no
gate is wired and every start is refused anyway). `boot.js` and `env.browser` do not change. No
guard pins `currentOutput()`'s members; around the seam, `checkTheContinuationsAndTheGatesWiring`
pins boot's two gates before the bar mounts and the arguments `output.js` hands `take`, `resume`
and `start`, and `TestThePlayerFrameIsWiredFromTheStreamToTheBar` pins boot's `wireOutput`; none of
them changes.

**What moves it.** Every reader action, before the request it makes:

| Reader action | Where | Moves it | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| A row's play, Shuffle | `playQueue` | yes | its start asks the gate, which takes the generation after the move |
| The bar's Play/Pause | `toggle()` | yes, whatever it does | over a resume of the browser's own waiting on the gate, which the bar shows starting, the click lets the wait go and starts nothing; where the bar shows Pause it pauses (`pauseNow`); where it shows Play it starts through the gate, or does nothing over an element that already plays, its play event still to come |
| The lock screen's Play | `toggle({ play: true })` | yes, where it starts | never pauses; keeps a resume's wait; nothing over an element that plays; otherwise a start through the gate |
| The bar's Pause, the lock screen's Pause | `pauseNow` | yes | records its pause; lets go of the starts the gate holds (`cancelStarts`) |
| Next, Previous, the bar's and the lock screen's | `advance` | yes | its start asks the gate |
| The lock screen's Stop | `clearQueue` | yes | lets go of the starts the gate holds |
| The playing row's removal | `removeAt`, the current row | yes | its successor is asked of the gate as a continuation, after the move |
| An output pick | `newPick` | yes | both slots dropped, as before |
| A pick of the bridge | `suspend` | yes | |
| A take, a switch-out's handover | `take` | yes | its own permit reads no generation, and its deferred play takes the generation after the move |

Nothing else moves it: a seek, the level, shuffle and repeat, an append, the removal of a row that
is not playing, every command and edit sent while the bridge is the output, the retry after an
error and the skip past a source that cannot play (continuations), and no event of the element:
`play`, `pause`, `ended`, `error`, `emptied`, `waiting` move nothing.

**What asks it.** Every request that starts this browser takes the generation as it is asked, and
every admission compares it with the generation now:

| Admission | Takes it | Where it moved |
|---|---|---|
| The switch-out's done, answered now (`switchOut`) | at the pick, after `newPick` | the output's move completes (this browser, its phase, the moving words cleared), and nothing is taken or started: in the branch its permit lets go on and in the one it refuses, which took the handover stopped |
| `recoverSwitchOut` | the switch-out's | the same: no `take`, no `gateStart` |
| `finishInherited`, a switch-out done | an inherited entry's: this document's at its boot, 0, or, where its stored record says the reader acted after it was sent, -1, which no generation here takes | the same: no `take` |
| The switch-in refused, and `resolveSwitchIn` | after `suspend` | output, phase and words as before; no `gateStart` of the resume |
| A start waiting for the epoch (`gateStart`, `flushWaiting`) | `waiting.reader` | let go at the flush, its drop called, nothing sent; a continuation displaces a waiting start the reader asked only once that start is stale |
| The barrier's slot (`decideStart`) | `startPending.reader`, taken again whenever the slot's run changes | a continuation takes a slot whose run is nulled or stale, the displaced entry's drop called; at the barrier's done, its unknown and its refusal for no player, a stale slot is let go as a nulled one is, quietly |
| `continueStart`'s go | as the continuation is asked | runs nothing |
| `ownPlay`'s go | at its play event | runs nothing, in place of the old count of actions |
| `loadAt`'s play at the metadata | at `loadAt`, after a take's move | plays nothing |
| `handleSourceError`'s skip | before the probe, carried through the 1,200 ms timer | the timer skips nothing |
| `permitFor` | none | reads no generation: a start's later plays keep their permit, a take's own included, and the next continuation of a take's playback goes on |

The barrier compares the generation of its slot's run, never that of the request that sent the
barrier: a Pause can let a row's start go and a resume of the browser's own made after it take the
same slot before the first POST answers, and that resume is the reader's last word.

**A Stop survives a reload.** Session storage keeps, with each operation not yet resolved, whether
the reader acted after it was sent (`acted`), apart from whether a newer pick superseded it
(`stale`), and `readerActed()` stores the identity again at every move; a reload reads an
inherited entry's generation from it, so the switch-out read back after a Stop and a reload takes
nothing.

**Pause events carry their origin.** The count of protective pauses becomes an ordered record of
the pauses the bar makes (`pauses`), each with why: the reader's (`pauseNow`), a protective one
(`ownPlay`), the bar's stop at the queue's end (`stopAtEnd`). Only a `pause()` that moves the
element to paused is recorded, since only it queues a `pause` event; each event takes the oldest
record; a load discards them all, as it removes their events, and queues no pause of its own. A
Stop's and a suspend's pauses are followed by a load, which removes their events, so they record
nothing. No pause event moves the generation:

| The pause event | The intent | A resume of the browser's own waiting, and its spinner |
|---|---|---|
| recorded, the bar's own | as its caller left it | kept |
| unrecorded, the element still paused and not ended: the browser's own, an interruption | off | let go |
| unrecorded, `ended`: a track's end fires `pause` before `ended` | kept: the advance and repeat-one go on | kept |
| unrecorded, a play overtook it: the element plays again | kept | kept |

**The control does what it shows.** The bar shows Pause where a play event came and the element
has not failed since, a fetch the user aborted (code 1) being no failure (`pauseShown()`); the
snapshot carries it (`pauseShown`) and the bar's label reads it, so the label and `toggle` read one
predicate. The bar's `toggle()`, the lock screen's Pause and its Play are three handlers, as the
first table says: the bar's alone shows the resume's spinner and cancels it on a click; the lock
screen's commands say exactly Pause and Play.

### Deferred, with their residuals

- The start guard's parser gaps: `withoutGatedCalls` blanks the body of a function literal handed
  to a gate call even where it is invoked at once, `stripJSComments` reads comment delimiters
  inside a string literal as a comment's, and `jsMaskLiterals` reads a regular expression after a
  keyword (`return /x/`) as a division. A start written in one of those shapes could pass
  `TestEveryStartOfThisBrowserAsksTheGate`.
- The bar's cancel of a resume of the browser's own that waits on its barrier lets the resume go,
  and the switch-out its ask sent still pauses the bridge's player when it lands.
